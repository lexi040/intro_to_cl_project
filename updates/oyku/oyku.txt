25 November 2026 (part 2)

- Fixed a small bug 
- Added MLE to src/bootstrap.py: MLE alpha and xmin from Ani's mle_alpha, plus a new mle_fit(verses) that does the same fit on resampled verses, so both least squares and MLE now get a 95% bootstrap CI (cache/bootstrap_alpha.csv). xmin is chosen again on every resample, so its uncertainty is part of the CI.
- Found that the MLE bootstrap is unstable: the MLE alpha often falls outside its own CI, worst when xmin is small (xmin = 1 for fi and tr ws_raw/ws_lower). Resampling verses changes the counts of rare words (a word in a verse drawn twice appears twice, a word in a verse not drawn disappears), and MLE with small xmin depends on exactly those counts. First tried with xmin fixed from the full corpus, then re-estimated on every resample: both were biased. Least squares with rmax = 500 stays inside its CI, so this is our argument for which estimate we trust.
